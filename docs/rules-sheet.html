Armory Lockdown: rules

Everything else, the game shows you. Hover before you click.

The goal

You are the intruder, alone with 8 HP and no way to heal. Security arrives in 3 waves: the first is there at the start, and you are not told when the others come.

Win when the last wave has arrived and every enemy is dead. Lose at 0 HP, or if round 30 ends with the fight undecided.

A round

  1. Your turn: 3 AP (action points) and 3 free movement. Move before, between and after actions; Sprint (1 AP) gives 2 more. Unused AP and movement are lost. Free moves can be undone until you spend AP or a move sets something off.
  2. Minions: each of yours acts on its own, oldest first, moving and then acting. You steer them only by where you put them.
  3. Enemies: each moves, then acts once, oldest first. An enemy that arrived this round only moves.
  4. Environment: the lab's defences act; poison and Parasite deal damage and poison spreads; eggs hatch; corpses decay.

Tiles, range and sight

  • Move one tile at a time, up, down, left or right. One ground unit per tile; flying units pass over units.
  • Range is the larger of the column and row difference, so diagonals count as 1. Adjacent means the 8 tiles around.
  • Sight is a straight line between tile centres; only walls and pillars block it. A projectile hits the first unit or blocking object on that line, yours or not. A lob ignores what is in between.
  • Shields and eggs block movement and projectiles, not sight. Mines, corpses, vents and panels block nothing.

Damage and statuses

  • Everything damages everything. Your mines, spores, chains and stray shots hurt you and your minions too.
  • Armor reduces kinetic, explosive and fire damage by its value. Electric, acid, poison and Parasite damage ignore it.
  • An enemy leaves a corpse for 2 rounds; your units leave none.
P3 Poison1 damage a round for 3 rounds. Each tick it spreads to unpoisoned neighbours (at most 3 new a round in the room), who get the rounds left.

Lab defences

The wall gun (H5), the floor panels (D5 and E5) and the gas vents (B5, G6) are the lab's, and its AI decides when each fires. A defence that fires hurts whoever is in reach, on either side.

It never fires without a cue first: The gun barrel glows. The floor panels crackle. A wisp rises from the vent. It shows at the end of a round; the defence fires (or stands down) at the end of the next, after your turn. Nothing shows which tiles are at risk.

Minions (at most 5: turrets, drones and hatchlings) only aim at enemies, but their blasts and chains hit anyone. Hatchlings: h basic, 2 HP, bites an adjacent enemy for 1; s spitter, 4 HP, 1 acid at an enemy within 3; b burster, 4 HP, explodes next to an enemy for 3 explosive to its tile and the 8 around. Any damage can break an egg (3 HP), and then nothing hatches. Medic and Warden corpses cannot hold one.

Reading the screen

  • Units. Yours are circles and squares (@ you, T turret, D drone, h s b hatchlings), enemies diamonds and a hexagon (G + F W). A shield badge is armor. Eggs and corpses (✕) show rounds left.
  • Intents (I). Every enemy and minion shows a dashed path to where it will go and an arrow to what it will hit. ▼3 is what a unit takes if you end the turn now, KO that it dies. A hollow ring with no arrow: arriving, so it will not attack. Hover a unit for its plan in words.
  • Previews. Pick an action and hover a target: −2 is damage after armor, +PSN +PAR +COR are statuses, KO a kill. A red dashed ring with ! marks your own units that would be hit.
  • The ▼ badges and the status strip's End turn now warning count minions and enemies, never the lab's defences.
  • Keys. 1–9 0 - = actions, Esc cancel, Tab grapple mode, Z undo, E end turn, F speed, Space skip, L intercom log.
